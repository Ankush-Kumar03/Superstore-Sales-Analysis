
# Superstore Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data to identify business trends, understand customer purchasing behavior, and evaluate profitability across different regions, categories, and segments.

The goal is to extract meaningful business insights using Python and data visualization.

## Objectives
- Analyze sales and profit performance.
- Identify the most profitable regions and product categories.
- Understand monthly sales trends.
- Analyze customer segments.
- Identify areas for business improvement.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Business Questions
1. Which region generates the highest profit?
2. Which product category contributes the most profit?
3. Which cities have the highest sales and profit?
4. Which customer segment generates the most sales?
5. How do sales and profit change over time?
6. Which sub-categories are most and least profitable?

## Key Insights
- The West region generated the highest profit.
- Technology was the most profitable product category.
- New York City was among the leading cities in profitability.
- Sales and profit varied across customer segments.
- September, November, October, and December showed significant profit performance.

## Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing regions and product categories.
- Investigate low-profit product sub-categories.
- Review discount strategies to improve profitability.
- Use monthly sales trends to plan inventory and promotions.
- Develop targeted strategies for different customer segments.
